# Walter's Project Budget

A simple app for tracking materials and costs on restaurant repair jobs, organized by store.

**Live app:** https://samuelfb3.github.io/walter-budget/

## Setup (one-time)

1. Open the link above in **Safari** on your iPhone (has to be Safari for this to work).
2. Tap the **Share** icon → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. You'll get an app icon on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.

## The dashboard

When you open the app you'll see four sections, one per store: **#302, #303, #304, #305**.

- Tap a store's name to fold/unfold its project list. The store's total cost still shows even when it's folded.
- Tap the **+** button (bottom right) to start a new project. You'll pick a store, name the job, and set a priority (High / Medium / Low).
- Inside each store, projects are sorted by priority automatically — High jobs float to the top.

## Inside a project

Tap any project to open it.

- Tap **+** to add a material: name, quantity, and unit price. The line total and the project's total cost update automatically.
- The **camera checkbox (📷)** next to each material is a reminder — check it off once you've actually taken a photo of the receipt and saved it (email it to yourself, keep it in Photos, whatever you normally do). **The app does not store the photo itself**, it's just a checklist so nothing gets forgotten.
- The **×** deletes a material. **Delete this project** at the bottom removes the whole job.

## Good to know

- The app works with **no signal** — you can use it in a walk-in cooler with zero bars.
- All your data is saved **only on this phone**, inside Safari. There's no cloud account and nothing syncs anywhere.
- Don't clear Safari's website data/history for this app, or your projects will be erased with no way to get them back.
- If you switch to a new phone, this data won't transfer automatically — there's currently no backup or export.
